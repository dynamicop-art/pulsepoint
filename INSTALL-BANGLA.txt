IMPORTANT LIMITATION
--------------------
OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / public Overpass services demo/prototype-এর জন্য suitable,
but they are not guaranteed emergency-grade infrastructure. Render Free service can also
sleep after inactivity, so first request may take around 30-60 seconds to wake.
For real production emergency use, use an always-on backend plus a supported commercial
places/healthcare data provider or your own maintained hospital registry.
