AETHER v8.1 Mobile Stability Hotfix
===================================
Fixes:
- Landing login/signup/admin cards collapsing into three narrow columns on phones.
- Student workspace inheriting desktop 112px left padding on phones.
- Desktop left dock leaking into mobile and clipping the bottom navigation.
- Utility strip overflowing horizontally instead of scrolling safely.
- Student header/nameplate being squeezed/truncated incorrectly.
- Smart Dashboard heading contrast on the dark AETHER surface.
- Cockpit/cards constrained to safe phone widths.
- Added stronger mobile safety rules for forms, cards, modals and canvases.
- Service worker changed to network-first for HTML/CSS/JS to prevent mixed stale deploys.

No Firestore rule change is required for this hotfix.
